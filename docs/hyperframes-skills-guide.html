product-launch-video
~/.agents/skills/product-launch-video
Marketing & Promo
The Strategic Advantage: Traditional SaaS promo videos take days of manual After Effects composition, keyframing UI mockups, and adjusting timing. product-launch-video automates the entire formula—hook, problem visualization, UI feature breakdown, social proof, and high-impact call-to-action—rendering a commercial-grade announcement video from a simple product URL or brief.

Ready-to-Use Prompts

agy, create a 30s product launch video for our app with feature highlights and a CTA
agy, generate a SaaS reveal promo from this product landing page URL
agy, build a commercial teaser for v2.0 highlighting speed improvements and new UI

Key Capabilities & Specs

  • Structured 6-beat marketing formula (Hook → Problem → Solution → Features → Proof → CTA).
  • High-converting kinetic typography and simulated browser/mobile frames.
  • Beat-matched audio bed integration with ducked voiceover narration.
Ideal Duration
15s – 60s
Output Aspect
16:9 / 9:16
general-video
~/.agents/skills/general-video
General Production
The Strategic Advantage: The ultimate architectural multi-scene engine. When your video doesn't fit into a rigid niche template (e.g. brand sizzle reels, multi-chapter presentations, product tours, or custom visual narratives), general-video orchestrates the entire scene timeline, coordinates sub-compositions, binds shared variables, and enforces strict seekable deterministic rendering.

Ready-to-Use Prompts

agy, create a 4-scene video explaining how our platform works with voiceover
agy, make a brand montage video with upbeat BGM and typography cards
agy, compose a 60-second video from my STORYBOARD.md plan

Key Capabilities & Specs

  • Full multi-scene orchestration using data-start and data-duration attributes.
  • Multi-track audio mixing with automated ducking and fade-in/fade-out envelopes.
  • Parameterized video rendering: update video copy and numbers via JSON without re-authoring.
Ideal Duration
30s – 180s
Architecture
Multi-Scene DOM
embedded-captions
~/.agents/skills/embedded-captions
Social & Subtitles
The Strategic Advantage: Modern viral short-form video demands dynamic, word-by-word animated subtitles. embedded-captions provides 35+ preset identities (Hormozi, MrBeast, cinematic punch-in, glow-trail). It runs locally end-to-end, supports subject matting to place animated text behind the speaker, and requires zero paid third-party transcription APIs.

Ready-to-Use Prompts

agy, add Hormozi-style animated captions to "C:\videos\clip.mp4"
agy, embed word-by-word karaoke subtitles behind the speaker in my video
agy, apply style-12 cinematic pop captions to my 9:16 vertical reel

Key Capabilities & Specs

  • 35 distinct visual identity presets ranging from subtle anchor text to high-energy pop.
  • Local Whisper transcription integration for millisecond-accurate word boundaries.
  • Foreground subject depth layer: text renders behind the speaker with no manual rotoscoping.

Key Capabilities & Specs

  • Procedural paper collage, tape overlays, and stop-motion style camera judder.
  • Automated document highlighting with handwriting/marker write-on animations.
  • Pacing grid optimized for rapid-fire visual revelations with subtle paper SFX.

  • Camera punch-in and framing automation to eliminate awkward pauses and emphasize points.
  • Works on 16:9 widescreen or reframes into 9:16 portrait for Instagram/TikTok.

~/.agents/skills/pr-to-video

The Strategic Advantage: Changelogs and git diffs are notoriously dry and ignored by users. pr-to-video reads a GitHub Pull Request or local git commit range, extracts the key changes, and builds an animated code-walkthrough video with syntax-highlighted diffs, file tree transitions, and narrator explanations—perfect for Twitter and release announcements.

Ready-to-Use Prompts

agy, explain the diff between main and feature-branch in a visual walkthrough video

Key Capabilities & Specs

  • Direct git diff parsing: automatically creates animated before/after code blocks.
  • Visual file tree highlighting modified components and architecture changes.
  • Natural narration explaining the motivation, architectural trade-offs, and user benefit.

p5-paint-animation
~/.agents/skills/p5-paint-animation
Artistic Generative VFX
The Strategic Advantage: AI video generators often fail at precise, artistic hand-drawing because diffusion models produce flickering artifacts. p5-paint-animation is 100% procedural code: it simulates real acrylic paint, watercolor washes, and pencil handwriting that draws itself onto the screen frame-by-frame with zero external video generation models or GPUs.

Ready-to-Use Prompts

agy, create a handwritten text animation that writes "Antigravity 2.0" in pencil strokes
agy, turn this photo into a painting that paints itself onto canvas over 5 seconds
agy, generate an artistic watercolor reveal animation for our title logo

Key Capabilities & Specs

  • Procedural brush engine (p5.brush): authentic textures, bristle splatters, and pressure.
  • Pencil & calligraphy handwriting effect that traces vector paths frame-by-frame.
  • Zero GPU requirement: renders cleanly and deterministically on CPU via Headless Chrome.

p5.js + Canvas
